DejaVu Sans / DejaVu Sans Mono fonts (regular + bold), vendored here so the
PDF report (app/routers/report.py) can render Unicode glyphs — currency (₹),
status marks (✓ ✗ ⚠), Greek/physics symbols (Δ ρ σ √ →) — that the base-14
PDF fonts (Helvetica/Courier) don't have.
